Goblin's Crypt

Game Instructions

  • Your objective is to defeat the goblin monsters and the final boss to win the game.
  • This is a turn-based game where the player and the monster will have one action per turn.
  • Both Player and Monsters/Boss can attack, use a power-up and drink a healing potion.
  • Monster actions are randomly selected.
  • Hope you enjoy the game!
